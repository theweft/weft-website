# weft brand

Everything here is generated by [generate.py](generate.py) (`just brand`) into
[public/brand/](../public/brand/), served at `https://theweft.org/brand/<file>`. Decisions, rejected alternatives and rationale:
[weft-design docs/19-brand.md](https://github.com/theweft/weft-design/blob/main/docs/19-brand.md).

## Parts

| Term | What | Files |
|---|---|---|
| Logomark (symbol) | The weave: two vertical warp threads, two horizontal weft threads, over and under | `mark-*.svg`, `mark-*-512.png` |
| Wordmark (logotype) | "weft" in Inter SemiBold, converted to outlines | `wordmark-*.svg` |
| Horizontal lockup | Logomark left, wordmark right | `lockup-horizontal-*.svg` |
| Stacked lockup | Logomark above, wordmark below | `lockup-stacked-*.svg` |
| App icon / avatar | Logomark on the light background, full square (platforms round the corners) | `app-icon.svg`, `app-icon-512.png`, `apple-touch-icon.png` (180 px), `github-avatar.png` (500 px) |
| Social preview | Horizontal lockup, 1200 × 630 | `og-image.png` |
| Favicon | Logomark that follows the browser's light or dark mode | `../favicon.svg` |

Each logo comes in four colourings: `light` (for light backgrounds), `dark` (for dark
backgrounds), `black` and `white` (one colour, for print, engraving and photos).

## Colours

| | Light backgrounds | Dark backgrounds |
|---|---|---|
| Warp (vertical threads) | `#1f3bad` | `#3355db` |
| Weft (horizontal threads) | `#c18215` | `#f6bb55` |
| Wordmark | `#1d2333` | `#e6e8ee` |
| Background | `#f7f6f2` | `#11151f` |

The two thread colours differ in brightness as well as hue, so the weave stays visible in
greyscale and for colour-blind viewers. Each colour has at least 3:1 contrast against its
background.

## Use

- **Minimum size:** logomark 16 px; horizontal lockup 20 px high.
- **Clear space:** keep free space of at least half the logomark's height around any logo.
- **Pick the variant for the background:** `light` on light, `dark` on dark, `black` or `white`
  on photos and busy backgrounds.
- **Do not** recolour the threads, rotate the mark, change the over and under, round the thread
  ends, add effects (shadows, gradients, outlines), or set "weft" in another font.
