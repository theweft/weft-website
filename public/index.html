weft

Weave existing open tools into one end-to-end embedded fleet platform.

Status: early design. Nothing to install yet.

What it is about

Provisioning, updates, configuration, workloads, messaging and data collection for fleets of embedded devices, from microcontrollers to gateways. weft integrates proven open tools instead of rewriting them.

  • Every layer is swappable Each layer sits behind an open standard. Keep your own broker, update tool or data pipeline and plug it in.
  • Offline by design Everything works without cloud or internet. Updates and configuration also travel as signed offline bundles.
  • Decentralized Authority comes from signing keys, not from a server. Devices verify what they receive and decide locally.
  • Permissive and in Rust MIT licensed, memory-safe, with a small trusted core that runs on Linux and microcontrollers.
